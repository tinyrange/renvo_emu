Known gaps

xtensa-lx7
Register evidence
45 registers across 34 required regions · manifest

Passing corpus

  • Pinned Espressif GCC qualification at -O0, -O2 and -Os
  • 1,000-case generated C corpus
  • CoreMark standard performance and validation workloads
  • Unmodified ESP-IDF hello_world

Known gaps

  • the LX7 interpreter is a compiler-baseline subset, not a complete Xtensa ISA model
  • windowed ABI, level-one exceptions, S32C1I atomics, and FPv5 compiler cases are tested without claiming cycle accuracy
  • interrupt priority/nesting, exact SMP timing, wireless, full USB, and PSRAM initialization timing are deferred
  • Peripheral time advances in deterministic abstract ticks rather than silicon clock cycles.
  • GPIO proof covers the low output bank and enable set/clear paths, not the complete GPIO matrix.
  • UART0 is transmit-focused and does not model physical baud timing.
  • Timer-group, RTC/ULP, GDMA and interrupt-matrix behavior is functional and deterministic rather than cycle accurate.
  • SYSCON radio-only clock/reset/configuration fields retain their documented register contract but have no radio side effects; radio behavior is tracked separately in issue #334.
  • USB-OTG covers deterministic full-speed device reset, setup receive FIFO/status, endpoint completion, native word-access masks, W1C interrupt fields, endpoint active/NAK status, documented seven-bit transfer-size fields, and host control/bulk exchanges; PHY edge timing, DMA scatter/gather, host channels, and complete USB classes remain outside the functional slice.
  • Wi-Fi/BLE radio support is intentionally deferred for a separate design; analogue inputs use deterministic host-supplied samples.
